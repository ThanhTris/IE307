## What changed from GM-07.A

GM-07.A remains historical and unchanged. B adds the owner-approved aggregate Top 3 result, score/ranking versions, locked dish-card projection (nullable image/price and display labels), host/settings/expiry/removalCap, caller-private preferences, per-member accepted submission status, minimal join preview, lobby settings/preferences mutations, and a finalize ACK that distinguishes round advance from terminal result. Latest R2 rule: N>=1 opens a real R2; at N=1 `removalCap=1` and the player may remove or not remove the sole dish. No auto-finalize and no synthetic KEEP for a non-submitter. Detailed behavior is in [the B contract](../../../architecture/DISH_SESSION_API_CONTRACT_B.md).

The result is computed from complete server-ACKed round-one ballots only: `2 × WANT + OK`; round-two KEEP adds nothing; NO/REMOVE candidates are excluded. Equal scores use dense shared ranks. Result selection and order are persisted once and remain stable through retry, reconnect, history and notification routing. Aggregate totals appear only after finalize to room members; raw ballots, individual contributions and seeds remain private. NO_CONSENSUS contains no winner or podium.
