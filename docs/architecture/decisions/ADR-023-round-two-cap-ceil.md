# ADR-023 — Round-2 removal limit rounds up

- Status: **Accepted by the project owner in chat on 2026-11-12** (to be re-confirmed by Trí in review).
- Scope: round 2 of new sessions on the live runtimes (B and B-supplement). Amends the cap formula of ADR-019 and ADR-020; everything else in those ADRs stays.
- Runtime: `0049_round_two_cap_ceil.sql`, applied to the local QA database only.

## Context

ADR-019 fixed the limit at `max(1, floor(0.30 × N))`, with N the dishes left after round 1. On a phone test, 6 dishes still allowed only one removal and 5 dishes only one, which felt too strict: the owner expects 5 dishes to allow 2.

## Decision

The limit per person is `max(1, ceil(0.30 × N))`, computed with integers as `(3N + 9) / 10`.

| N | 1–3 | 4–6 | 7–10 | 11–13 | 14–16 | 17–20 |
|---|-----|-----|------|-------|-------|-------|
| limit | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

- Removing nothing stays valid; the limit is a ceiling. N = 1 still allows one removal.
- Unchanged: no automatic KEEP for people who did not submit, no refilling, no round 3, `ALL_REMOVED` when every dish is removed.

## Where it applies

- SQL (all four places that computed it): `dish_v4_private.view` (`removalCap`), `dish_v4_private.dispatch` and `gm20_canonical_private.dispatch` (`REMOVE_LIMIT` on submit), `gm20_canonical_private.evaluate_v4` (the pure decision model).
- Client: `getRoundTwoEliminationCap`, the snapshot guard that checks `removalCap`, and the domain evaluator `removalCapV4`.
- Fixtures: the `cap-NN-*` vectors of `tests/fixtures/decision-v4/cases.json` are regenerated by `mobile/scripts/regen-cap-vectors.mjs`.
- Sessions already in round 2 when the migration is applied get the new limit on their next submit; the snapshot and the server check use the same function, so they cannot disagree.

## Evidence

- Jest (`voteRepository.test.ts` cap table 1–20, `roundTwoView.test.tsx`, parity tests) and `node --test mobile/tests/dishPolicyV4.test.mjs` (109 vectors).
- Not run: SQL/domain parity CI (`scripts/dish_v4_ci.py`, `scripts/gm20_canonical_check.py`), which needs its own QA project.
- Older evidence documents (GM-15, GM-26, audits) still show the previous formula; they describe the state at their date.
